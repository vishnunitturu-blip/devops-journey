DAY 9 – Services & Process Management

Commands learned:
ps
ps -ef
top
kill <PID>
systemctl status <service>
systemctl start <service>
systemctl stop <service>
systemctl enable <service>

Learnings:
- Processes are running programs with unique PIDs.
- ps and top help monitor system activity.
- kill is used to stop stuck processes.
- systemctl manages background services in Linux.
